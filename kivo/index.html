About KIVO

The web should feel free, personal, and under your control.

Our vision is to bring together modern browser technology, meaningful privacy protection, and thoughtful design into one unified browsing experience. We are building KIVO for users who value a clean interface, greater control over their browsing experience, and a browser that adapts to their needs.

Privacy from First Principles

No tracking, metrics pings, cloud accounts, or usage collection without explicit consent. Your typed omnibox queries and history are evaluated locally with zero keystroke transmissions.

Personalization Without Bloat

Zero sponsored shortcuts, zero injected advertisements, and zero cryptowallets. Customize your digital clock, wallpaper library, shortcuts, and themes seamlessly.

Product Capabilities

Engineered for Everyday Elegance

Every component is crafted for speed, ergonomics, and uncompromising safety.

Integrated Ad & Tracker Shields

Native network-level blocking stops tracking cookies, analytics telemetry, and malicious miners before requests hit the network.

Multi-Tab Browsing Architecture

Multi-tab memory optimization, tab re-opening (`Ctrl+Shift+T`), rapid switching, and sandboxed execution per view.

Secure Password Vault

Protected local credential storage, intelligent form autofill, and encrypted multi-device vault synchronization.

Smart Local Omnibox

Calculates search suggestions, open tabs, bookmarks, and history locally on your device with zero keystroke telemetry.

Aesthetic Personalization

Custom wallpapers, customizable shortcut tiles, 12h/24h digital clocks, and instant dynamic dark/light appearance.

KIVO Cloud Synchronization

Optional, user-controlled synchronization for bookmarks, settings, and credentials backed by modern Supabase architecture.

Privacy Principles

Our Commitments to You

Privacy is not an add-on feature; it is the foundation of KIVO's product vision.

01

Respect for User Privacy

No tracking, selling, or monetization of your personal browsing history or habits.

02

Local-First Browsing

Omnibox queries, suggestions, and history calculations remain strictly local on your device.

03

Transparent Data Handling

Plain-language controls for permissions, shields, cookies, and network requests.

04

Protected Credentials

Zero plain-text password storage on disk or in transit. Local AES-256 and OS keychain security.

05

Isolated Private Browsing

Incognito sessions completely isolate caches and cookies, destroying them upon window exit.

06

User-Controlled Synchronization

Complete autonomy to decide what synchronizes across devices and what stays offline.
